One of them

What does this print?

x=5
x+=1
echo "$x"

51

On an ordinary variable, += appends a string: "5" followed by "1". Arithmetic needs an arithmetic context — ((x+=1)) or x=$((x+1)) — or a variable declared with declare -i.
