Ratings

Everyone is rated on the same nineteen topics

A rated attempt is scored like a match against the lab: pass inside the limit and you beat it; fail, or run over, and it beats you. Ratings are Glicko-2, so every number carries its own uncertainty — a profile says 1500 ± 350 until it has earned the right to be more precise.

A rating comes from rated labs and rated theory. Those are graded by the server against your account. An unrated attempt is still recorded — it shows on your profile with its score and its time. No rated lab has been published yet, so today the board is empty of real players; the sample accounts are marked. Why the catalogue is split.

Sample data. Nobody here is real. These 100 accounts are generated by seed/generate.py and replayed through the same rating code the live service runs, so the pages can be judged before anyone has signed up.

How the number moves

The lab is the opponent

Difficulty 1 to 5 plays at 1100, 1300, 1500, 1700 and 1900. Beating a difficulty-5 lab is worth what beating a 1900 is worth.

The clock counts

5, 10, 15, 20 or 30 minutes by difficulty — unless the lab sets its own, as the exam simulation does. Finishing late is a loss, however good the fix.

Practice is free

Untimed attempts are recorded and never rated. Retry a lab as often as you like; only the timed run says anything about you.
