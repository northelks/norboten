What is new

Every new lab, journal and question bank is announced in the server the day it lands on main, and every release with its notes. Nothing is posted by hand, so nothing is missed.

Channels by topic

Somewhere to ask about a lab you are stuck on, compare fixes once you have passed, or talk about the subject itself. Please keep a lab's solution to yourself until others have had a go.

Reach the author

A lab that is wrong, a check that passes when it should not, an idea for the next one: say it in the server, and it reaches the person who wrote it.

Linking Discord to your Norboten account is separate, and optional: it lets the weekly digest arrive as a direct message. It is on the account page, and the privacy policy says what it stores. If you would rather support the project another way, there is Donate.
